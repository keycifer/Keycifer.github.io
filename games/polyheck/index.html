:POLYHECK:

Welcome to PolyHeck! Can you beat the 27 stages and beat PolyHeck?

PolyHeck is an early 80s arcade styled game where you must kill every enemy to progress to the next stage. Some stages may even offer different objectives.

PolyHeck is also a 2 player co-op game. Play locally with your friend! (online multiplayer soon)

Features

  • 27 levels
  • 5 areas (StoreFront, Faculty Num, Stone Temple etc)
  • 2 playable characters
  • local multiplayer
  • 5 bonus levels
  • difficulties from easy to berzerk
  • toggable powerups/items
  • score saving
  • full screen support
  • toggable keybinds
