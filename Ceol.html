Back in 2021-2022, the music for the old PolyHeck games was made with a program known as Bosca Ceoil.


Due to me (Plain) being an inexerpienced and young person... and not having any money to hire an actual talented musician-


I downloaded Bosca and made music with it since I didn't know what program to get due to programs such as FL Studio seeming too daunting (and because it costs money-). Bosca worked well with the type of games I was making anyways since they had the pixel art style and were very amaturish.


This page serves as an archive for all the ceol files from this "retro era" that I was able to recover. Unfortently not many of the old music were recovered, but one day I will look at my laptops old harddrive and hopefully recover more!


♦If you want to listen to these files, download Bosca Ceoil Blue here, a rewritten updated version of the original program by yurisizov. Alternatively, you can download the original link here, which is what I used back in the day.♦
